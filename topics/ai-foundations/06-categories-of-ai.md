<h3>Part 1: The 5 Core AI Job Categories</h3>

<div class="lead-in-card">
  <span class="lead-in-badge">5 CATEGORIES</span>
  <p>Learn the 5 main ways AI helps in an office.</p>
</div>

<p>1. **Summarization (Shortening):** Turning long notes or documents into quick bullet points.</p>

<p>2. **Generation (Creating):** Writing brand-new draft messages, outlines, or ideas.</p>

<p>3. **Analysis (Finding Patterns):** Looking through feedback notes to spot common themes.</p>

<p>4. **Transformation (Re-formatting):** Changing text tone, fixing grammar, or converting list formats.</p>

<p>5. **Automation (Connecting Steps):** Connecting tools so repetitive actions run automatically.</p>
