<h3>Part 1: The 5 Core Workplace AI Categories</h3>

<div class="lead-in-card">
  <span class="lead-in-badge">USE CASE TAXONOMY</span>
  <p>Every workplace AI task fits into one of 5 distinct operational categories.</p>
</div>

<p>1. **Summarization:** Condensing long documents, transcripts, and reports into bulleted takeaways.</p>

<p>2. **Generation:** Creating new written content, outlines, emails, and visual diagrams.</p>

<p>3. **Analysis:** Identifying trends, anomalies, and insights across datasets or feedback logs.</p>

<p>4. **Transformation:** Reformatting data, translating languages, or rewriting tone.</p>

<p>5. **Coding & Automation:** Writing formulas, scripts, and connecting web triggers.</p>
