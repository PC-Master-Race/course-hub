<h3>Part 1: Cloud AI vs Local AI</h3>

<div class="lead-in-card">
  <span class="lead-in-badge">PRIVACY OPTIONS</span>
  <p>Local AI runs entirely on your computer so your information never leaves your desk.</p>
</div>

<p>**Cloud AI (like ChatGPT or Claude):** Sends your typing over the internet to big computer servers.</p>

<p>**Local AI (like Ollama or LM Studio):** Runs AI models directly inside your laptop. **Zero data leaves your machine.**</p>

<p>Local AI works completely offline without internet, keeping confidential work 100% private.</p>

| Feature | Cloud AI (Internet) | Local AI (Offline on Laptop) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Where Data Goes | Sent over internet to server farm | Stays 100% inside your computer |
| Internet Needed? | Yes ✅ | No ❌ (Works offline without Wi-Fi) |
| Private Files Safe? | Requires paid corporate account | Yes ✅ (Zero information sent outside) |
| Computer Needed | Any phone or basic browser | Requires modern laptop with good memory |
