<h3>Part 1: Advanced Prompting Techniques</h3>

<div class="lead-in-card">
  <span class="lead-in-badge">PRO TECHNIQUES</span>
  <p>Layer advanced prompting techniques on top of RTCF to unlock exceptional output quality.</p>
</div>

<p>**Few-Shot Prompting:** Provide 1 or 2 high-quality examples in your prompt so the AI learns your exact style and format.</p>

<p>**Chain-of-Thought (CoT):** Force the AI to reason step-by-step before producing its answer (e.g., 'Think step-by-step before answering').</p>

<p>**Persona & Constraint Prompting:** Set strict boundary rules (e.g., 'Do not use jargon', 'Limit to 150 words', 'Adopt an empathetic tone').</p>

<h3>Part 3: Generative Image AI & Multimodal Workflows</h3>

<div class="lead-in-card">
  <span class="lead-in-badge">MULTIMODAL AI</span>
  <p>Generate professional diagrams, mockups, and assets using visual AI tools.</p>
</div>

<p>Multimodal AI accepts both text and images as inputs or outputs.</p>

<p>When prompting image models (Midjourney, DALL-E, Imagen), specify subject, style, lighting, aspect ratio, and composition.</p>

<p>Use image AI for mockups, presentation visuals, and flowcharts—never for authentic factual photography.</p>

| Visual Element | Vague Image Prompt | Professional Image Prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Workplace Mockup | 'A picture of an office.' | 'A modern, bright workplace interior, minimalist design, natural sunlight through large windows, 4k photo, 16:9' |
| Diagram | 'A workflow chart.' | 'A clean, modern vector flowchart showing 4 customer service steps, vibrant blue and teal accents, white background' |
