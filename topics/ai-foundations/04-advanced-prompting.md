<h3>Part 1: Advanced Prompt Techniques</h3>

<div class="lead-in-card">
  <span class="lead-in-badge">NEXT STEPS</span>
  <p>Layer these simple bonus techniques on top of RTCF to get even better outputs.</p>
</div>

<p>**Giving Examples (Few-Shot Prompting):** Show the AI 1 or 2 past examples of good work so it sees the exact style you want.</p>

<p>**Step-by-Step Thinking (Chain-of-Thought):** Tell the AI to 'think step by step out loud' before answering. This helps prevent silly mistakes.</p>

<p>**Setting Constraints:** Give clear rules about what NOT to do (e.g., 'Do not use hard technical jargon', 'Keep under 100 words').</p>

<h3>Part 3: Creating Images with Visual AI</h3>

<div class="lead-in-card">
  <span class="lead-in-badge">VISUAL AI</span>
  <p>Learn how to write simple descriptions to generate clean diagrams and image mockups.</p>
</div>

<p>Visual AI tools create pictures from text descriptions.</p>

<p>When describing an image, list 4 details: **Subject** (what is in the picture), **Style** (photo, vector, drawing), **Lighting** (bright, warm), and **Layout** (wide, square).</p>

<p>Use visual AI for presentation diagrams and slide mockups.</p>

| Visual Item | Vague Description | Detailed Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Office Image | 'A office picture.' | 'A modern, bright office interior, clean desk, natural light, high-quality photo' |
| Diagram | 'A flowchart.' | 'A clean vector flowchart showing 3 customer service steps, blue color theme, white background' |
