Now that you know how to move around the Excel grid, let's learn how to put information into the cells. Excel can hold text (labels) and numbers (values).

---

## How to Enter Data

Entering information is simple:
1. Click on the cell where you want to put data (make it the active cell).
2. Type your text or numbers on the keyboard.
3. Press **Enter** on your keyboard. This saves your entry and moves the green box down to the next cell.

> [!TIP]
> If you make a mistake while typing, press the **Backspace** key to erase. If you've already pressed Enter, simply click back on the cell and type over the old content, or double-click the cell to edit a single letter.

---

## Formatting for Readability

To make your spreadsheet look clean and professional, you can format the text:
- **Bold Titles**: Select your header cells and click the **B** (Bold) icon on the Home tab ribbon.
- **Center Text**: Select the cells and click the Centered text alignment icon in the ribbon.
- **Adjust Column Width**: If text is too long, you might see it cut off. Move your mouse pointer to the line between two column letters (like between A and B) until it turns into a double-headed arrow, then double-click. Excel will automatically resize the column to fit your text!

## Practice Steps

Let's build a simple shopping list:
1. In cell **A1**, type `Item Name` and make it **Bold**.
2. In cell **B1**, type `Price` and make it **Bold**.
3. In cell **A2**, type `Apples`. In cell **B2**, type `2.50`.
4. In cell **A3**, type `Milk`. In cell **B3**, type `3.75`.
5. Adjust column A's width so the text fits comfortably.
